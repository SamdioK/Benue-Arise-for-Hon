POLICY & PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY

A practical agenda
for Benue.

Core policy areas proposed for public discussion.

01

Security and rule of law

Discuss community safety, early warning, emergency response, access to justice and support for displaced residents. Specific commitments need operational plans, budget estimates and legal review.

02

Agriculture, enterprise and jobs

Explore safer access to farms, agricultural inputs, storage, processing, market connections and small-business support.

03

Education and youth opportunity

Consider school infrastructure, teacher support, vocational learning and digital skills.

04

Healthcare and social welfare

Prioritise primary healthcare, maternal and child health, essential medicines and health-worker support.

05

Infrastructure and rural development

Review roads, water, energy access and project selection, with public timelines and progress reporting.

06

Transparent institutions

Promote open procurement, measurable service standards and responsible public finance.

These are discussion topics, not final promises. Add official proposals and costings before describing them as campaign commitments.
