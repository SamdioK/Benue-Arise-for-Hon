Privacy notice

How public enquiries and website interactions should be handled.

Information submitted

If the contact form is enabled, the name, email, enquiry type and message you submit may be processed to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive personal information.

Purpose and retention

Enquiry data should be used only for the stated communication purpose, access-limited, retained only as needed and deleted under an approved retention policy.

Security and your choices

Production use requires server-side validation, spam controls, restricted database access and a verified contact for privacy requests. This starter notice must be reviewed and adapted by the site operator before collecting live submissions.

Cookies and external services

Supabase authentication and storage may process technical information when enabled. Review the relevant provider notices and configure only the services the site actually uses.
