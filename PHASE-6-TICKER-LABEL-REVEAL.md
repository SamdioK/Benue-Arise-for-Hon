# Phase 6 — Ticker Label Reveal and Crystal Shine

- The orange “BENUE ARISE UPDATE” label now occupies space only while visible.
- When it collapses, the scrolling welcome/news text expands into the vacated space instead of leaving a blank block.
- The label cycle is 20 seconds: it is visible for approximately 5 seconds and remains away for approximately 15 seconds before returning.
- On reappearance, a bright diagonal crystal-like reflection sweeps across the orange label.
- Mobile screens use a narrower 132px label width and matching keyframes.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable the animations and keep the label and message readable.
- The main ticker continues its 52-second loop.
- Changes are committed to implementation/brand-and-foundation; no live deployment was made.
